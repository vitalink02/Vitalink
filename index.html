How the score works

MATH

Pulse changes with motion and effort.

Heart rate responds to the intensity of a task. The higher the effort, the larger the pulse response and the greater the energy cost.

Your body uses energy = intensity × time to power movement and recovery.

Cells convert fuel into usable energy.

At the cellular level, oxygen and nutrients are processed to release ATP, which powers movement, repair, and regulation.

Cellular respiration: oxygen + glucose → ATP + water + carbon dioxide

One climb

See how movement changes your score.

Climbing a staircase is a simple way to estimate effort, energy use, and recovery demand in real time.

Mechanical work

Your body lifts weight against gravity as you climb. More steps means greater work and more energy used.

Recovery response

Your heart and lungs respond quickly. This creates a clear signal of effort and recovery speed.

Energy estimate

When height, step count, and body mass are included, you can estimate oxygen demand with a useful practical model.

Habit signal

Repeated stair work or active movement adds up, improving cardiovascular fitness and daily energy.
